# Welcome to the Guide

Fent is a good asset to bring to zones, wars, or in general shoot outs. Why? Because you get **50 health and 50 armor** after popping one. However, it is very expensive to craft.

## 2. Crafting Fentanyl
Now to turn it into fent. Here is the expensive and long recipe:
* **2 Stamina Potions** + **2 Cleaning Kits** + **4 Magic Mushrooms** + **2 Stoned Energy Drinks** + **5 Ketamine Bags** $\rightarrow$ **6 Fentanyl**

### Material Sources:
* **Stamina potions:** Any pharmacy store
* **Cleaning kits:** Any mechanic shop
* **Stoned energy drinks:** Any weed store
* **Mushrooms:** Location 553
* **Crafting Bench:** Hippy place over at Paleto
  <img src="https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/1559980753817833563/1559981424264749096/MedalTVGrandTheftAutoVFiveM20260828011413079.png" alt="fentbenchpostal" /> 
  <img src="https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/1559980753817833563/1559981424856141916/MedalTVGrandTheftAutoVFiveM20260828011354956.png" alt="fentbenchspot" />

### Tips & Market Value
* **Optimal Batch Size:** A batch to make 900 fent requires 750 bottles, 500 ketamine Oz, 500 scales, 600 mushrooms, 300 kits, 300 energy drinks, and 300 stamina potions.
* **Selling:** You cannot sell these to locals (only ketamine, and locals only pay 300 so it's not worth it). However, you can sell them to other players since this is a top-tier healing item to match ifax and is more convenient than gfax/blunts.
* **Warning:** If you are trying to tap into this market, be aware that people are allowed to scam you out of your drugs 😄
